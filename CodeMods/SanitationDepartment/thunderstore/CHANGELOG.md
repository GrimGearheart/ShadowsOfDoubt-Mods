# Changelog

## 1.1.1

- Added a "Safe to remove?" note to the description. No changes to the mod itself.

## 1.1.0
- New: **crime scene cleanup.** Get certified at City Hall. While you're on sanitation full-time, residents are found dead around the city; the Enforcers investigate, then release the scene to you. Bag the body, mop up the blood pool and scrub the spatter, and you're paid when it's clean. The coroner collects the body and the landlord clears out the flat once you've left.
- New: mop and bucket at City Hall (scrubs a wider area, faster).
- New: a Credentials card on the pause menu showing your licenses, tools, bag and any open cleanup jobs. Shared with Detective License.
- Blood pools, wounds and body bags are never treated as trash.
- The full-time and back-to-detective options are now at the top of the City Hall list.

## 1.0.0
- Initial release: Sanitation License, trash and junk tags, paid binning, a 15-item bag (30 as Route Supervisor), dustbin collection with refills, bulky junk you can toss in or break down, litter picker, Route Supervisor glow, and an optional full-time mode with no new murders (sandbox only).
